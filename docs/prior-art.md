# Focused comparison, 28 October 2026

This is a focused primary-source screen, not an exhaustive priority determination. The theorem compared is the scalar-valued alternating cubic recovery theorem in `manuscript/main.tex`, with arbitrary supported error and an unknown field. It has not been replaced by a random-instance or quantum-circuit claim.

Contribution language: the particular supported-corruption theorem, its two-translate cubic specialization, complete compatible certificate, and bounded costs. Do not describe determinant forms, adjoint equations, underlying-field recovery in general, or differential cancellation as newly invented here.
